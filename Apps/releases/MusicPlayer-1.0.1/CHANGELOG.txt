Music Player 1.0.1
==================

Distribution:
- Convert the current modular Music Player into an official signed NeutronOS App Store package.
- Preserve current MusicPlayerManager behavior and controller-first UI.
- Preserve library cache, playlists, downloaded/extracted artwork cache and settings across uninstall/reinstall.
- Keep MusicPlayerManager compiled support OS-owned.

Dependency contract:
- mpv is the only App Store Debian dependency.
- MusicPlayerManager explicitly refuses playback when the mpv executable is unavailable.
- ffmpeg/ffprobe are already provided by the NeutronOS base image.
- PipeWire, WirePlumber and ALSA tools are already provided by the NeutronOS base image.
- QtQuick and QtQuick.Controls are already NeutronOS base runtime modules.
