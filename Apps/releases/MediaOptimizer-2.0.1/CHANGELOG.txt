Media Optimizer 2.0.1
=====================

Distribution:
- Convert the current modular Media Optimizer into an official signed NeutronOS App Store package.
- Preserve the approved Media Optimizer UI and MediaOptimizerManager behavior.
- Preserve settings, presets and scan cache across uninstall/reinstall.
- Keep MediaOptimizerManager compiled support OS-owned.

Dependency contract:
- No App Store Debian dependencies.
- ffmpeg and ffprobe are used by MediaOptimizerManager and are already installed by the NeutronOS base-image builder.
- QtQuick and QtQuick.Controls are already NeutronOS base runtime modules.
- No package lifecycle scripts or package-owned system services are added.
